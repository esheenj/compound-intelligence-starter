    3. Read the limits before acting

    A source marked “declared verified” is still just a claim supplied in JSON. Even a match to a reference does not prove that the action follows from the evidence. “8/10 sample outcomes” is a self-reported count, not an 80% confidence level, probability of success or calibrated model quality score.
