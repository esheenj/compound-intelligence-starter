3. Interpretation limits

Rates and usage are self-supplied; the price-book revision is just a label. This measures neither quality nor latency, and it does not represent provider billing, discounts, caching, taxes or actual spend. Different model workloads can make direct cost comparisons misleading.

The browser calculates integer-scaled fixed-point USD values with BigInt, preserving up to twelve decimal places. An unobserved model is rejected, not ranked as free. All input is processed locally and never transmitted.
